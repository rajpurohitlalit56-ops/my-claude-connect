Support

Common sawaalon ke jawab, ya seedha humse baat karein
Payment karne ke baad access kab milega?
Screenshot verify hone ke baad turant access mil jata hai — usually kuch hi minutes mein. Agar zyada time lage, Contact Us se sampark karein.
Invite link kaam nahi kar raha?
Invite link ek baar use ke liye hota hai. Agar expire ho gaya ho, apna order ID ke saath Contact Us se sampark karein — naya link mil jayega.
Payment ke liye kaunse tareeke available hain?
Har product page par QR code diya hota hai — kisi bhi UPI app se scan karke payment karein, phir screenshot bhej dein.
Kya refund ya batch swap ho sakta hai?
Verify hone ke baad payment final hai — course swap ya refund possible nahi hai. Order se pehle sahi batch/book confirm kar lein.
Screenshot upload nahi ho raha?
Sirf JPG/PNG format allowed hai. Agar phir bhi issue ho, page reload karke dobara try karein ya Contact Us se batayein.

Aur madad chahiye?

Payment, order, ya kisi bhi issue ke liye humse seedha baat karein.
